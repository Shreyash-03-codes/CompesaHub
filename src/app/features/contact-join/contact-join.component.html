Eligibility to Join COMPESA

  • Enrolled in the Computer Engineering program at our institution
  • Active participation in technical events and workshops
  • Good academic standing with a minimum CGPA requirement
  • Commitment to contribute at least 2 hours per week
  • Passion for technology, innovation, and peer learning

Applications are invited at the start of each semester. Watch the News section for announcements.
